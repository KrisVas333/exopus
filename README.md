# Exopus · ExoClass

Concept landing page for **Exopus**, the AI helper the ExoClass team is building on top of ExoClass, ExoSend and ExoSign. Sold as a team member you hire, not another system you buy. Meta Muse-inspired: a character in its own little world, a live "working on…" line, a task console, and an approval step before anything goes to parents.

Live: https://krisvas333.github.io/exopus/

## What's on the page
- **3D octopus that transforms per activity** (three.js, `3d.js`): football, robotics, martial arts, dance, coding, languages, art, basketball, music, gymnastics, critical thinking, chess, science. Each gets a costume built from simple shapes, an exact brand tint on the skin only, and its own way of moving. Tentacles ripple per arm in a vertex shader. The hero cycles through them until someone picks one.
- **Models:** Meshy (4 looks from Sep 23 + Linas's character via image-to-3D), optimized with gltf-transform (meshopt + WebP, ~0.4-0.7 MB each). Unrigged, so all motion is procedural.
- **Style studio** (bottom-right button): 5 characters × 14 activities × light/dark. The URL keeps the combo: `?s=linas&a=dance&t=light`. Add `&studio=0` to hide the panel for outside viewers.
- **Brand:** exoclass.lt DNA. Plus Jakarta Sans, #3097D7 → #AE4197 (gradient #239FDC → #C4318B), #CCE0F0, ink #2E2B33, ExoClass logos from exoclass.lt. ExoSend and ExoSign have no public logos, so they appear as product names.

Status: concept, 2026-09. Not a live ExoClass feature. Demo data is illustrative.
